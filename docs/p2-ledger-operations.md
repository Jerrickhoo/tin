## Storage and transactions

Migration `007_financial_operations.sql` retains the historical four entry kinds and signed legs. `ledger_groups` owns immutable financial metadata, typed links and one-successor uniqueness. Each leg mirrors that same metadata as `financial` JSON. Legacy groups are backfilled with null metadata; notes never classify refunds or principal. Recognized legacy opening-balance notes retain their existing onboarding/pace meaning.

`ledger_operations` records stable command identities and exact payloads. `apply_financial_operation` authenticates the owner, locks that owner's ledger, checks predecessor snapshots, signs, group structure, typed ownership links, correction ancestry, recovery/settlement caps and safe aggregates, then posts all legs, status changes, obligations and audit in one transaction. Replays cannot add money. Direct financial metadata inserts and financial status updates are denied to clients; old outboxes can still insert/void legacy rows within the new guards. New tables use owner RLS and read-only client grants.

Dexie v4 adds obligations, audit and a financial journal. A financial save writes its mirror effects and one outbox command atomically. Queued dependent commands support offline save/Undo and partial repayments. A definitive server rejection rolls back that command and its dependent tentative effects, retains complete drafts as conflicts, and resets the pull cursor for an authoritative refresh. Ambiguous network failures retain the same command identity for retry. Discarding a financial queue item also rolls back tentative effects; a server pull resolves an ambiguous prior acceptance.

Corrections keep the original active while editing, preserve exact untouched timestamps and original income proportions by default, and save one linked replacement. History sorts the terminal version by effective date and collapses earlier versions; search and pagination retain whole chains. Superseded originals cannot be restored. Linked origins cannot be corrected/voided until their related state can be coherently resolved; this implementation blocks unsupported relinks rather than silently rewriting them. Payment void/restore recalculates outstanding and rechecks caps. Standalone voids have a reviewed permanent Restore action.

## Financial behavior

All current REST/RPC requests carry `X-Client-Info: tin-ledger/1`, including replays of pre-P2 outbox payloads. Migration 007 refuses ledger inserts/updates from older cached clients so their void-then-reenter Fix flow cannot mix with the atomic protocol. Refresh old clients before enabling financial writes. This is a compatibility guard; RLS and RPC validation still provide authorization. PostgREST exposes the lowercased request header through its [transaction-scoped request settings](https://docs.postgrest.org/en/stable/references/transactions.html#request-headers-cookies-and-jwt-claims). The staging gate must verify the header survives the real Supabase gateway.

- Shared projections expose ordinary income, gross purchase spending, returned money, net spending and adjustments. Refunds/reimbursements lower net spending on receipt day, including negative net on return-only days. Principal and transfers stay outside purchase spending/income. Category breakdowns and pace are explicitly gross; adjustments have separate lines and do not reset the top-up anchor.
- Refunds normally select an original purchase. An explicit unknown historical origin is stored as unknown, without inventing a purchase; its recovery cap cannot be verified. A shared-purchase claim reserves recovery eligibility and creates no cash movement. Receivable/payable principal shares the same capped settlement core. Historical opening debt is non-cash and keeps its chosen effective date. Excess requires a separate classification. Non-cash claims/opening records can be canceled once active payments are voided.
- Account transfers have opposing Cash/Online tags in one explicitly selected envelope. The entry preview shows source balance and negative outcomes. Envelope transfers retain their separate meaning. Actual negative balances remain visible.
- Manila date entry is independent of device timezone. Chosen dates without known times use noon; untouched original instants retain seconds. One reporting clock refreshes at Manila midnight/resume. Monday/Sunday preference works offline, syncs to the profile after the financial migration, and protects newer local choices from stale responses. Home and History share calendar-week boundaries; History retains a separate rolling-seven-day preset.
- Reconciliation reviews observed/expected/delta/as-of, explicit envelope ownership and reason. Known missing activity opens a normal entry draft. Unknown differences use an adjustment or zero-sum untagged classification. Equal balances create audit only. New ledger activity invalidates a preview. Legacy targets are references and no longer override Home's ledger balances. Settings exposes last-checked dates and an optional in-app reminder.

## Backup and lifecycle

JSON format 2 includes tags, metadata/links, obligations and audit. Version 1 remains readable and its `money_type` is preserved. New financial restores are atomic, validate references/caps/ancestry and reject identity collisions. CSV adds movement/account labels and operation/origin/correction identities. Imports and recurring postings retain ordinary income/purchase meaning and local duplicate-group idempotency. New tables, pending preferences and user-scoped keys participate in owner switch, logout and account reset; reset counts include repayment records and audit.

An older server cannot silently drop tags or turn a classified receipt into ordinary income. New financial writes require `financial_capabilities() = 1`; a previously verified account can save offline. Missing migrations retain the draft/queue with a fixed update-needed message. Do not strip metadata to make a rejected push succeed.
